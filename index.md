# Planning Framework

Rules for a requirements repo's product side — charter, initiatives, epics, milestones, product requirements, and
mockups — its engineering side — engineering requirements, assumptions, specs, and slices — and the decisions both
share, each rule carrying a stable, citable `planning:<slug>` id.

Read [`./context/index.md`](./context/index.md) when working in or changing a planning or requirements repo, or to
resolve a cited `planning:` id to the file that owns it.
