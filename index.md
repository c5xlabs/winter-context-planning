# Planning Framework

Rules for a requirements repo: its product side (charter, initiatives, epics, milestones, product requirements,
mockups), its engineering side (engineering requirements, assumptions, specs, slices), and the decisions both share.
Each rule has a citable `planning:<slug>` id.

Read [`./context/index.md`](./context/index.md) when working in or changing a planning or requirements repo, or to
resolve a cited `planning:` id.
